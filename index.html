How to use this page
  1. Start with the companies below. Tap the status pill to move a partner along — Not started, Talking to them, Shortlisted, Appointed. Type the company name in the line beside it.
  2. Then work the list. Tap any task to tick it off. Tap again to untick.
  3. Use the five step buttons to focus. Tap “2. Pick partners” to see only what is live now. Tap it again to show everything.
  4. Use the area buttons to split the work. Tap “Outside” to give the Projects Lead their list, “Coffee & food” for the House Manager, and so on.
  5. Nothing needs saving. Every tick is stored on this device the moment you make it. Close the tab and come back whenever — it will be exactly as you left it.
Progress is kept in this browser, on this device. If someone else opens the page on their own laptop they will start from a blank copy. Use Back up progress at the foot of the page to hand a snapshot to someone else.
